Here’s a short 2027 roadmap for 360Medicare, built around Revenue and IT Automation.

Basic (Q1, foundation)

Finish the 360M App and retire Zoho CRM, with one login and one source of truth.
Auto lead capture from Meta Ads, the website and WhatsApp, with every lead tagged by source and department.
Auto lead assignment and follow-up reminders, so no lead sits untouched.
Daily and monthly reports sent automatically (Lead Status Tracker, ROI, weekly report for Muneer).
Automated email and WhatsApp sequences: new lead, event invite, no-response nudge.
Simple role-based access for each department and manager.

Deep (Q2-Q3, revenue engine)

Cost per lead, cost per conversion and ROI by campaign, channel and department, live on a dashboard.
Lead scoring that ranks leads by how likely they are to convert.
Zoho Books-style invoicing and payment tracking inside the app, with automated payment reminders.
Study Abroad pipeline: Lead → Student profile → Application, with auto status updates to students.
Event automation (Chai with Migration, GERICON): invite, RSVP, reminder, follow-up, attendance, conversion.
Document collection portal where clients upload their own documents, with automatic checklists.
Recruitment ATS with a CV reader and auto-matching of candidates to jobs.

Futuristic (Q3-Q4, AI layer)

Expand AI calling (Tidyhire) to qualify leads, book consultations and call back no-shows.
AI WhatsApp assistant that answers FAQs 24/7 in English, Arabic and Malayalam.
AI that summarises each call and drafts the follow-up message for the consultant.
Predictive dashboard: forecast next month’s leads, conversions and revenue.
Auto Meta Ads optimisation: pause weak ads, scale winners, alert on cost spikes.
AI content engine for ad copy, event emails and social posts (e.g. alkalma Instagram).
Client self-service portal and mobile app with application status, payments and chat.
Anomaly alerts: if leads drop, a consultant is idle or a campaign overspends, the right manager gets notified.
