Cost per lead, month by month

AED per lead = that month's marketing spend ÷ leads that reached the CRM. The small number under each month is its leads. In February only 866 leads reached the CRM. For March, Meta counted 4,560 leads (AED 3.66 each, shown above). The CRM received 3,103, so March is AED 5.39 here. I use the CRM count for every month so the months can be compared.

Cost per lead is going up

AED 4.14 in April, AED 11.13 in October. Spend more than doubled (AED 14,649 to AED 32,442) while leads fell 18% (3,537 to 2,915). Most of it is Migration: AED 3.60 per lead in April, AED 21.89 in October.

Where a client costs least

Recruitment AED 278 and 360Future Academy AED 296 per client. Migration AED 652, Licensing AED 797. Study Abroad AED 3,454: 5 clients from 2,813 leads.

Newer leads are still being worked

April leads have had 6 to 7 months to close. October leads have had under 2. The cost per client for Aug – Oct can still fall as more of them close.

Migration

7,798 leads, 106 clients (1.36%). AED 8.86 per lead and AED 652 per client on AED 69,070 of spend. It also brought AED 956,177 of the revenue in these months.
